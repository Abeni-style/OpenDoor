# Premium and student safeguards

OpenDoor is intended for school-age users, including minors. The Premium display may be shown as a planned offering, but checkout remains disabled by default.

Do not activate payment collection until an appropriate adult/organization:

1. owns and administers the merchant/payment account;
2. checks whether the payment provider supports the merchant's country and the customers' countries;
3. reviews age restrictions, consumer-protection obligations, taxes, privacy, refunds, cancellations and recurring billing;
4. publishes transparent pricing, terms and support contact details;
5. confirms that the product offers genuine additional value and does not paywall essential opportunity facts;
6. tests successful, failed, cancelled, expired and refunded subscription states;
7. approves any billing eligibility changes through a protected server-side process.

Never collect card details directly in OpenDoor. Use hosted checkout only. Do not enable billing for a minor or change `billing_eligible` from the public browser. If the adult/legal/merchant checks cannot be completed, leave Premium as a non-transactional coming-soon plan.
