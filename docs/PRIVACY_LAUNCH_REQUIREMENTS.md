# Privacy and student-safety requirements before public accounts

This is a practical launch checklist, not legal advice or a substitute for jurisdiction-specific review.

Before allowing students, including minors, to create cloud accounts:

- Publish a plain-language privacy notice explaining what is collected, why, where it is stored, who can access it, provider disclosures and how long it is retained.
- Publish terms, community rules, reporting/contact information and an account/data deletion process.
- Determine age and parental/guardian consent obligations for each country in which the service operates.
- Minimize profile fields. Do not require exact date of birth, home address, passport, government ID, school records or sensitive family/financial details for opportunity matching.
- Keep chat history off by default unless there is a clear need and a disclosed retention policy.
- Disclose when content is sent to an AI provider and review its data processing, retention and age requirements.
- Restrict moderator/admin access; use multi-factor authentication where available; log administrative changes.
- Set retention periods for sign-in records, feedback, chat service logs and source records.
- Test export/deletion requests and document how backups age out deleted data.
- Have an adult/organization owner review the service before accounts or paid services are enabled.

Until these requirements are completed, use the static demo and local browser storage. Do not market cloud account features as live.
